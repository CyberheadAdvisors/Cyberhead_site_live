What this usually looks like

Renewal asks what controls you have

At renewal, an insurer asks about your controls: multi-factor sign-in, patching, backups, who has admin rights. The answers are worth having to hand before the form arrives.

NCSC and IASME say certified organisations are 92% less likely to make a cyber insurance claim. That is a correlation reported by the provider of the included insurance. It is not a promise about your firm.

The £25,000 cover

Eligible organisations get cyber insurance with a total liability limit of £25,000 included with Cyber Essentials certification, arranged by IASME. Eligible means UK-based, turnover under £20m, the whole organisation certified, and opted in during the application.

It comes with certification. It does not come from Cyberhead, and we do not arrange it. IASME also describes a 24-hour incident response helpline that comes with the insurance. The cover is described for Cyber Essentials certification. If you choose Cyber Essentials Plus, ask IASME what applies.
