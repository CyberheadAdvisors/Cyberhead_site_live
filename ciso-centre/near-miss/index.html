What this usually looks like

Turning a warning into a fix list

If you have had a near miss, such as a convincing email someone almost acted on, you have a rare thing: a warning with no damage. The risk is that it fades, and nothing changes.

The gap assessment looks at the five Cyber Essentials controls and what your business shows to the internet. You get a short fix list for your IT team or IT firm, most important first.

The five controls are firewalls, secure settings, user access, malware protection and security updates.
