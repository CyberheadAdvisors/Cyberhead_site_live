Privacy notice

This site uses no cookies and no tracking. This notice covers the personal data you share when you book a call or email us.

Who we are

Cyberhead Advisors Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 14593535. Contact: sean.muirhead@cyberhead.co.uk.

What we collect and why

When you book a call or email us, we receive your name, email address, company and anything you tell us. We use it to reply, hold the call and, if you become a client, deliver and invoice the work. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, and contract once you are a client.

Business contact details from public sources

We collect business contact details (name, job title, work email address and company) from public sources, such as company websites and Companies House. We use them to contact firms about Cyberhead's services. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in telling businesses about a service that may help them. We keep these details for 24 months from our last contact with you, or until you object, whichever is sooner. To object, reply "no thanks" to any email from us, or email sean.muirhead@cyberhead.co.uk. We will then stop contacting you and keep only your name and email address on a suppression list, so we do not contact you again.

Who we share it with

Our email and booking tools are provided by Microsoft. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 24 months. Client records are kept for 6 years for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing us. If you are unhappy with our reply, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
