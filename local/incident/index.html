What this usually looks like

When the board asks what changes

If you have had a security scare, the next question from the board is usually the same: what are we doing about it? The honest answer is often a list that lives in people's heads.

The readiness check turns that into one page the board can read, and a fix list in order of importance for your IT team or IT firm. Cyber Essentials gives it a clear standard to work to.

If personal data was involved, you may need to report it to the ICO. Take advice on that.

What this is not

This is not incident response. If something is happening now, call your IT firm and your insurer first. Come to us when you are ready to look at what comes next.
